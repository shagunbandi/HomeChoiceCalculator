Here's a structured breakdown of the **input fields**, **output fields**, and their respective **formulas** from your `utils.py` and `main.py` files.

## **Input Fields**
These are the user-provided values used for calculations.

| Field Name             | Description                                    | Unit  |
|------------------------|--------------------------------|-------|
| `buying_cost`         | Property purchase price       | €     |
| `down_payment`        | Initial down payment          | €     |
| `sell_price`         | Expected selling price        | €     |
| `one_time_expense`   | Upfront costs (e.g., fees, taxes) | €     |
| `interest_rate`      | Mortgage annual interest rate | %     |
| `term_years`        | Mortgage term length          | Years |
| `yearly_maintenance` | Annual maintenance cost      | €     |
| `tax_credit_rate`    | Mortgage tax deduction rate  | %     |
| `current_rent`      | Monthly rent                 | €     |
| `rental_increase`   | Annual rent increase         | %     |

## **Output Fields**
These are the computed values displayed in the application.

| Field Name                         | Description                                          | Formula |
|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `monthly_payment`                   | Monthly mortgage payment                            | \(\text{Principal} \times \frac{r(1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}\) where \(r = \frac{\text{annual interest rate}}{12 \times 100}\), \(n = \text{term_years} \times 12\) |
| `total_payments`                    | Total amount paid over mortgage term               | \( \text{monthly_payment} \times (\text{term_years} \times 12) \) |
| `total_interest`                    | Total interest paid                                | \( \text{total_payments} - \text{loan_amount} \) |
| `loan_amount`                       | Mortgage loan amount                               | \( \text{buying_cost} - \text{down_payment} \) |
| `tax_credit`                        | Tax benefits from mortgage interest               | \( \text{total_interest} \times \left(\frac{\text{tax_credit_rate}}{100}\right) \) |
| `maintenance_total`                 | Total maintenance cost over term                  | \( \text{yearly_maintenance} \times \text{term_years} \) |
| `capital_gain`                      | Net profit/loss from property sale                | \( \text{sell_price} - \text{buying_cost} \) |
| `total_buying_cost`                 | Final cost of buying                              | \( \text{buying_cost} + \text{total_interest} + \text{one_time_expense} + \text{maintenance_total} - \text{tax_credit} - \text{capital_gain} \) |
| `total_rental_cost`                 | Total cost of renting over term                   | \( \sum \text{monthly_rent} \) with compounding increase, where \(\text{monthly_rent}_{\text{year } y} = \text{current_rent} \times (1 + \frac{\text{rental_increase}}{100})^y \) |
| `breakeven_month`                   | Month where buying is cheaper than renting       | First month where \(\text{Cumulative Cost (Buying)} < \text{Cumulative Cost (Renting)}\) |
| `net_monthly_cost_buying`           | Monthly net cost of buying                        | \( \text{Interest} + \text{Maintenance} - \text{Tax Credit} \) |
| `cumulative_cost_buying`            | Running total of buying costs over time           | Cumulative sum of `net_monthly_cost_buying` |
| `cumulative_cost_renting`           | Running total of renting costs over time          | Cumulative sum of `Monthly Rent` |
| `cost_difference_rent_buy`          | Difference between total renting and buying cost  | \( \text{cumulative_cost_renting} - \text{cumulative_cost_buying} \) |

---

## **Breakeven Point Calculation**
The breakeven month is found by iterating through each month and comparing the cumulative costs:

- **Buying cost:**  
  \[
  \text{total_buying_cost} = \text{interest paid} + \text{maintenance} + \text{one-time expenses} - \text{tax credit} - \text{capital gain}
  \]
- **Renting cost:**  
  \[
  \text{total_rental_cost} = \sum \text{monthly_rent}
  \]
- The breakeven occurs when:  
  \[
  \text{cumulative_cost_buying} < \text{cumulative_cost_renting}
  \]
